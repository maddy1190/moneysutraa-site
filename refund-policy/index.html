Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please review this policy before completing your webinar registration. We aim to keep our policies simple, transparent and fair.

Refunds

Generally non-refundable after registration.

Duplicate Payments

Eligible for review and refund.

No Shows

Not eligible for refund.

Support

WhatsApp and Email assistance available.

1. Webinar Fee

The registration fee is charged for access to the selected educational webinar session. Registration is confirmed only after successful payment.

2. Cancellation by Participant

Once a seat is confirmed, the webinar fee is generally non-refundable. Participants may contact support in case of genuine payment or access-related issues.

3. Duplicate Payment

If you are charged more than once for the same registration, the duplicate payment will be reviewed and refunded after verification.

4. Technical Issues from Our Side

If the webinar is cancelled or cannot be conducted due to technical or operational issues from our side, participants may be offered a refund, rescheduled session, or alternate access.

5. No-Show Policy

If a participant does not attend the webinar after successful registration, the registration fee will not be refunded unless specifically communicated otherwise.

6. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds will generally be processed to the original payment method within 7–10 working days, subject to payment gateway and banking timelines.

7. Payment Support

For payment, refund, or webinar access issues, please share your registered name, phone number, email address and payment reference for verification.

Important: Webinar registrations are generally non-refundable. If you experience duplicate payments or a technical issue from our side, please contact support for assistance.

Need Help?

Contact the webinar support team for duplicate payments, refund verification or joining-link related queries.
