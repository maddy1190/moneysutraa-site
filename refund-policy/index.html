Refund & Cancellation Policy

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to paid online webinars, workshops, and educational sessions conducted under MoneySutraa.

Webinar Fee

The webinar registration fee is charged for access to the selected online educational session.

Cancellation by Participant

Once a seat is confirmed, the webinar fee is generally non-refundable. However, participants may contact us before the webinar for support in case of genuine payment or access-related issues.

Duplicate Payment

If a participant is charged twice for the same webinar registration, the duplicate payment will be reviewed and refunded after verification.

Technical Issue from Our Side

If the webinar is cancelled or cannot be conducted due to a technical or operational issue from our side, participants may be offered a refund, rescheduled session, or alternate access, as applicable.

No-Show Policy

If a participant does not attend the webinar after successful registration, the fee will not be refunded unless specifically communicated otherwise.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds, if any, will be processed to the original payment method within 7–10 working days, subject to payment gateway and bank timelines.

Contact

For payment, refund, or webinar access support, please contact MoneySutraa through the contact details provided on our website.

Note: MoneySutraa webinars are intended for financial education and awareness only. They do not constitute personalized investment advice, legal advice, tax advice, or any guarantee of returns.
