Refund & Cancellation Policy

This page explains how refunds, duplicate payments, no-shows, and webinar cancellations are handled for paid online webinars, workshops, and financial education sessions.

For paid webinar registrations

Please review this policy before completing payment for any online webinar or workshop.

Applies to online sessions

1. Webinar Fee

The webinar registration fee is charged for access to the selected online educational session. Registration is considered confirmed only after successful payment through the approved payment gateway.

2. Cancellation by Participant

Once a seat is confirmed, the webinar fee is generally non-refundable. However, participants may contact the support team before the webinar in case of genuine payment, duplicate payment, or access-related issues.

3. Duplicate Payment

If a participant is charged twice for the same webinar registration, the duplicate payment will be reviewed and refunded after verification of the payment details.

4. Technical Issue from Our Side

If the webinar is cancelled or cannot be conducted due to a technical or operational issue from our side, participants may be offered a refund, rescheduled session, or alternate access, as applicable.

5. No-Show Policy

If a participant does not attend the webinar after successful registration, the fee will not be refunded unless specifically communicated otherwise before the session.

6. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds, if any, will be processed to the original payment method within 7–10 working days, subject to payment gateway and bank timelines.

7. Support for Payment Issues

For payment, refund, or webinar access support, participants should share their registered name, phone number, email ID, and payment ID or screenshot for verification.

Note: This policy applies to educational webinars and workshops. It does not cover any personalized advisory, investment management, or financial product transaction.

Need help with payment or access?

Contact the webinar support team for duplicate payment, refund verification, or joining-link related queries.
