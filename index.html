Our Services

Goal-Based Planning

Turn your dreams—home, children’s education, retirement—into a clear, actionable money plan. We map each goal to practical SIPs and smart lump sums, then create horizon-wise glide paths that gradually shift from growth to safety as your deadline nears. No jargon—just a step-by-step route so you always know how much to invest, for how long, and when to de-risk.

Portfolio Review

Know exactly what you own and why. We scan your portfolio for hidden risks, fund overlap, and category/style drift, then streamline excess holdings and costs. Finally, we align each investment to the right benchmark with a clear rebalance plan and tax-aware fixes, so your money works harder with less noise.

Ongoing Rebalancing

Stay invested, not stressed. We run rules-based check-ins on your portfolio, nudging it back to target weights when markets drift—no gut calls, just a disciplined playbook. You’ll get clear, actionable notes (what moved, what we’re changing, why), so every tweak feels calm, timely, and tax-aware.
