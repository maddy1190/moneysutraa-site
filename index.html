ABOUT US

Who we are

MoneySutraa exists to reach as many people as possible and help them get their personal finance right — whether or not they ever invest through us.

We take an investor-first approach: anyone who reaches out gets a goal-planning session, no strings attached. If you want continuous, ongoing support — regular reviews, rebalancing, course-correction as life changes — we become your investment partner, because that kind of support only works if we can actually track your portfolio over time. Our approach is sequence-first: get the essentials in place — health insurance, term insurance, and disciplined SIPs — before anything else. Once you have a solid core mutual fund portfolio, we help you graduate to equity for higher, long-term growth. Build the foundation first, then chase the upside.

TRANSPARENCY

How we're compensated, and how you're protected

How we earn

We earn distributor commission (trail) from AMCs on the funds you invest in through us — not fees charged to you. Ours is an investor-first approach: we don't recommend a fund because it pays more; our process is documentation-led, not commission-led.

Stay alert to fraud

We will never ask you to pay, transfer, or hand over cash/cheques in an individual's name. All transactions happen through official AMC/RTA platforms. Report anything unusual to us immediately.

Your protection

We operate under ARN-336976 and follow AMFI's Code of Conduct for Distributors. You can escalate any unresolved grievance to SEBI via SCORES.
