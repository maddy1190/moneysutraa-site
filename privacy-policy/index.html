Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is collected, used, protected and handled when users visit this website, register for webinars, make payments, or communicate with the support team.

For website visitors and webinar participants

Please review how your information may be used for registration, payment confirmation, support and communication.

Applies to website & webinars

1. Information We Collect

We may collect basic information such as name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, city, webinar registration details, payment status, communication preferences, and information voluntarily submitted through forms or support channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to manage webinar registrations, verify payments, send confirmation messages, share webinar access details, provide support, send reminders, maintain internal records, and communicate relevant educational updates.

3. Payment Information

Payments for paid webinars, workshops, or educational sessions may be processed through approved third-party payment gateway partners. This website does not store sensitive card, UPI, net banking, or banking credentials.

4. Communication Consent

By registering for a webinar, submitting your details, or contacting support, you may receive communication through email, phone, WhatsApp, SMS, or similar channels related to registration, payment confirmation, reminders, access details, support, and educational updates.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers such as payment gateways, webinar platforms, email/communication tools, technology providers, or where required by law, regulation, dispute resolution, or operational necessity.

6. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no online system, transmission method, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. User Rights and Corrections

Users may contact the support team to request correction, update, or deletion of their personal information, subject to legal, operational, payment, tax, dispute-resolution, and record-keeping requirements.

8. Cookies and Basic Analytics

This website may use basic cookies, analytics tools, browser/device information, or similar technologies to understand website performance, traffic sources, page usage, and user experience. Users may control cookie preferences through their browser settings where applicable.

9. Third-Party Platforms

Webinar access, payments, communication, email delivery, analytics, and support may involve third-party platforms. Users should also review the privacy practices of those platforms when interacting with them.

10. Children’s Privacy

The website and webinars are intended for adults and general financial education audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If such information is submitted, please contact support for assistance.

11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the website or participation in webinars after updates means the user accepts the revised policy.

Educational Disclaimer: Webinars and workshops are intended for financial education and awareness only. They do not constitute personalized investment advice, legal advice, tax advice, or any guarantee of returns.

Need privacy or support assistance?

For privacy-related queries, payment support, or webinar access issues, contact the support team.
