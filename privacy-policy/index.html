Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains how MoneySutraa Fin Services LLP collects, uses and protects your information when you use our calculators, contact us on WhatsApp, register for webinars, make payments or reach our support team.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

What We Collect

Name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, and details you choose to share.

How We Use It

To reply to you, share your estimate, give guidance and run our webinars.

WhatsApp Messages

Only with your consent. Reply STOP anytime to opt out.

Your Rights

Ask us to correct or delete your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, city, webinar registration details, payment status and any information you choose to share through forms, WhatsApp or our support channels.

When you use our life insurance, health insurance or investment calculators, the numbers you enter stay in your browser. We do not save them unless you choose to send them to us, as described in section 2.

2. Calculators and WhatsApp

Our calculator pages offer two optional ways to get in touch:

  • Message us on WhatsApp. The button opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message that includes your estimate. Nothing is sent until you review the message and press send yourself.
  • Leave your WhatsApp number. If you enter your number in the "Not ready to chat?" box, we save it together with the calculator you used, your estimate and the date, so we can send you your estimate, related tips and information about insurance or investments.

Numbers left on our calculator pages are stored in a Google Sheet that only MoneySutraa can access, and may be added to our WhatsApp messaging account with AiSensy so we can reply to you.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to respond to your enquiry, share your estimate and relevant guidance, help you choose and apply for insurance or mutual fund products, process webinar registrations and payments, send confirmations, reminders and access details, provide support, and send relevant educational communications.

If you choose to apply for a policy or investment through us, we share the details needed for that application with the relevant insurer, intermediary or mutual fund, as the application requires.

4. Communication Consent and Opting Out

By leaving your number, registering or contacting us, you consent to receiving related communication from MoneySutraa by WhatsApp, phone, email, SMS or similar channels.

You can stop WhatsApp messages at any time by replying STOP, or by asking us on WhatsApp or by email. We will then stop sending you promotional messages.

5. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through approved third-party payment gateway providers. Sensitive banking or card credentials are not stored on this website.

6. Cookies, Analytics and Advertising

This website uses cookies, analytics tools and similar technologies to understand traffic and performance.

Our calculator pages use the Meta Pixel. It tells Meta (Facebook and Instagram) when someone visits a page, uses a calculator, taps a WhatsApp button or leaves a number, so we can measure our ads and show them to relevant people. The amounts you enter and your phone number are not sent through the pixel. Meta handles this data under its own privacy policy, and you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

7. Third-Party Services

We use trusted service providers to run our services, including Google (Google Sheets, for storing numbers left on our calculator pages), Meta (the Meta Pixel and WhatsApp), AiSensy (WhatsApp messaging), payment gateways, webinar hosting and email delivery providers. Each handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. It may be shared only with the service providers listed above, with insurers, intermediaries or mutual funds when you choose to apply through us, or where required by law.

9. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, or as long as the law requires. If you ask us to delete your number or details, we will remove them from our records, unless we must keep them for a legal or regulatory reason.

10. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

11. Your Rights

You may ask to see, correct, update or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent to messages, subject to legal and operational requirements. To do so, contact us using the details below.

12. Children's Privacy

This website, our calculators and our webinars are intended for adults.

13. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of this website indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

Educational Disclaimer: Our calculators and webinars are for educational and illustrative purposes only and should not be considered personalised investment, insurance, tax, legal or financial advice. MoneySutraa is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-336976). Insurance is offered through a certified Point of Sales Person (POSP) under IRDAI guidelines.

Questions or Requests?

For privacy questions, to stop messages, or to ask us to correct or delete your information, contact us.
