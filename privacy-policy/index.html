Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MoneySutraa collects, uses, and protects information shared by users through our website, webinars, workshops, forms, and communication channels.

Information We Collect

We may collect basic information such as name, email address, phone number, city, webinar registration details, payment status, and communication preferences.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to manage webinar registrations, send payment confirmations, share webinar access details, provide support, send reminders, and communicate relevant educational updates.

Payments

Payments for paid webinars, workshops, or educational sessions may be processed through third-party payment gateway partners. MoneySutraa does not store sensitive payment card, UPI, or banking details on its website.

Communication

By registering for a webinar or submitting your details, you may receive communication from MoneySutraa through email, phone, WhatsApp, SMS, or other available channels related to the webinar, workshop, or educational services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with trusted service providers such as payment gateway providers, webinar platforms, email/communication tools, or where required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information. However, no method of online transmission or storage is completely secure, and users should share information carefully.

User Rights

Users may contact MoneySutraa to request correction, update, or deletion of their personal information, subject to legal, operational, and record-keeping requirements.

Educational Disclaimer

MoneySutraa webinars and workshops are intended for financial education and awareness only. They do not constitute personalized investment advice, legal advice, tax advice, or any guarantee of returns.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, payment support, or webinar-related assistance, please contact MoneySutraa through the contact details provided on our website.
